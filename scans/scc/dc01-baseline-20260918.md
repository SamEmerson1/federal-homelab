## Why DC01 scores higher than WS01

A domain controller exposes more services than a workstation, so the intuitive expectation is a
lower score. The opposite result comes from the denominator: SCC scores pass / (pass + fail), and
Not Applicable rules are excluded from both. DC01 has 21 N/A rules against WS01's 5, plus 29
unanswered manual checks against WS01's 10 — 50 of DC01's 291 rules fall outside the calculation.

The Server 2025 STIG is also a first-release benchmark (V1R1), while the Windows 11 STIG is at
V2R10 and has accumulated more automated checks over its revision history.

Raw failure counts are the more honest comparison: 138 on DC01 versus 148 on WS01, similar in scale.
